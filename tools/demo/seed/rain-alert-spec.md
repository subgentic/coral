# Skyline: rain alert spec

A person can ask Skyline to warn them before rain.

## Behaviour
- The alert fires when the chance of rain in the next two hours is 60 percent or more
- One alert per city per day, so a wet afternoon does not buzz all day
- The alert says the city, the hour and the amount ("Lisbon, 15:00, light rain")
