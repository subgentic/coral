Review notes
  1. A notice of access already granted. This card is not a request to approve the grant. One item per relayed grant; scope, level and expiry come from the computer’s grant record. Dismiss hides the notice in this browser only and does not revoke access.
  2. Revoke here, by the local Operator. The computer’s own Coral offers Revoke with a confirmation naming the person, Hub and team. These cards appear only in Coral opened directly on this computer; they are not shown through a Hub. The build must enforce this in a separate local, humanInputCaller-protected route, never through the tunnel or agent/API-key paths.
  3. Supplied names are plain text. Hub, device and person are inserted with textContent, stripped of control/bidirectional formatting, capped at 40 display characters, and redacted for credentials and email addresses. Hub addresses show their origin only; key/person identifiers are shortened to eight characters. These display rules do not identify or authorize the action; the grant’s stored id does.
  4. Lifecycle. A failed revoke must leave the card and grant active with an error and Retry. Revoked/expired states offer Dismiss only. Other grants and links are unaffected. Dates, names and ids are samples.
  5. Mock first. No real grant is created or revoked. MemberRelayedOpen stays shut. Security must review the local-only action and notice feed before any gate flip; the implementation should match this approved mock.
