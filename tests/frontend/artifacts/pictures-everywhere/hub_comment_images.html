Coverage for approval

Every prose box: comments and edits, review notes and reasons, Inbox replies, questions, agent and team chat, new and edited goals/tasks, completion reports, instructions, reminders, scheduled prompts, notes and knowledge. Documents insert a durable image reference at the caret.

Names and titles: an adjacent attachment action adds a picture to the containing record. Passwords, searches, addresses, paths, IDs, flags and executable commands keep their validated text values; images do not replace those values. This boundary is part of the proposal.

Hub prerequisite: binary uploads, image reads, saved references and delivery must work together on the chosen computer. The finished UI offers pictures locally and through the Hub. The earlier proposal to hide the controls is held.
